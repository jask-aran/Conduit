# Heartbeat

How the heartbeat works for you. It runs when something new reaches jbot's
inbox (mail from a feed, a report from a run), at most once an hour and never in
quiet hours. It does not decide what you hear; jbot does. Its job is to make
sure jbot has the whole picture: it reads each new entry and, where the entry
alone is not enough, goes to the source for the rest. Edit this to change how.

## Look closer at

For anything that might matter, get what I would otherwise open the email for:
the tracking number and the link to track it, the order number and what was
ordered, the amount and the due date, who is asking and for what. Entries carry
their links as numbered references ([1] and so on): pass the right ones on.

Then look for context around it, in the rest of the mail and the calendar:

- A shipping notice that does not say what is in it: find the order it
  belongs to (an eBay or Amazon confirmation, or the shop's own) by the sender,
  the tracking or order number, or the last few weeks of order mail.
- A bill, renewal or account warning: the amount, the due date, what happens
  if it is missed, and any earlier notice about the same account.
- Mail from a person, not a company, that asks for something: the thread
  before it.
- Anything with a date or a time in it: what else is on the calendar then.

## Leave as they are

Still reported, in a line, but not worth digging into:

- Newsletters, promotions and marketing.
- Receipts for something already paid.
- Security notices for a sign-in I just made.

## Checklist

Gone through every few hours (Checklist every, in Settings), even when nothing
new came in. While this lists anything, each sweep is a model run; something
with a source to watch is better as a feed.
