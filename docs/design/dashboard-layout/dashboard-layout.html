Project and workspace dashboards

One layout for both, split about 65/35. The left column is the composer with the threads list directly under it, at the chat composer's width, so what you type and what you can open share one edge. On a workspace the list switches between Chats and the threads Conduit does not own. The right column is what is live and what changed: Running, Terminals, then Changes or Files. Both columns fill the pane and scroll on their own. On a phone the same parts stack in one column. The mockups use your data from 25 Sep, at the app's own type sizes. The terminal rows and the −/+ counts are examples.

What the phone changes

  • One column in the order Running, the threads list with its switch, Terminals, then Changes or Files. The page scrolls instead of the columns.
  • The composer docks at the foot, as it does in a chat, over a short fade to the pane colour, so rows under it never show through the glass.
  • Shortcuts become four large targets under the header, icon over a full word. This is the one place they carry icons, because a thumb needs a bigger target than a word.
  • Rows use the sidebar's phone sizes: 14.5px titles in 42px rows. Today the dashboard keeps its desktop 10.5px titles on a phone.
  • Track stays visible, since touch has no hover.
  • One ⋯ menu, the app header's. Today a phone shows a second one on the dashboard.

How it fits the pane

Measured with a container query on the dashboard's pane, not the window, so opening the workspace panel reflows it the same way a smaller window does.

Two columns pane ≥ 760px

The left column grows to 608px, the composer's width, and the right takes the rest, at least 240px. That is 63/37 at full width and about 65/35 with the workspace panel open. Each column scrolls on its own.

One column below 760px, phones

The phone order. On a desktop the composer stays at the top. On a phone it docks at the foot.

The parts

The numbers match "Show parts" above. A part with nothing in it is left out, except the threads list, which says "Nothing here yet."

  1. 1

    Header

    The glyph and name, its kind, and one muted line: path, branch, and how far ahead it is. There is no copy icon, since clicking the path copies it. Rename, identity, settings and delete live in the pane header's ⋯.

  2. 2

    Shortcuts

    Plain words at the header's right, over the right column: Changes, Files, Terminal, Settings on a workspace, and Search chats, Files, Copy path, Settings on a project.

  3. 3

    Composer

    The frosted composer, unchanged, heading the left column at the chat composer's width. A send takes it straight down to the chat's foot.

  4. 4

    Threads

    Directly under the composer and filling the column. On a workspace a switch in its heading flips between Chats and Not in Conduit, each with its count. The menu beside it sorts Chats or filters Not in Conduit by harness.

  5. 5

    Running

    Agents mid-turn, with the trace header's verb and time. It tops the right column and is left out when nothing runs.

  6. 6

    Terminals

    Workspaces only. Live shells with the current command in mono and how long they have run, each with the runtime dot. It is left out when none are running.

  7. 7

    Changes or Files

    A Git workspace shows its changes with the workspace panel's −/+ counters, per file and summed in the heading. A project, or a workspace without Git, shows recently changed files.

  • It shares the Chats slot. The switch shows both counts, so you can see there are 150 without the list taking a column of its own.
  • One list, newest first, with each row's harness mark. The menu beside the switch filters to one harness and shows each harness's count.

What they look like today

Captured on 25 Sep from the local server at 1280 wide with the workspace panel open, at 1600 wide, and at 384 × 832 on a phone.

What the captures show

  • The rail outgrows the list. The right column stacks to twice the height of the workspace's two chats. Columns that fill the pane and scroll on their own fix this.
  • Only Codex is asked. The dashboard shows five Codex sessions, while the server knows 150 threads in ~/Conduit across four harnesses.
  • The phone gets desktop type: 10.5px row titles and 9px captions.
  • Space goes on nothing. Whole tiles say "No live terminals." and "No untracked Codex sessions."
  • Old material. Hairline tiles, heading strips whose subtitles repeat the title, and a chevron on every row.

Kept from the redesign doc

  • First open. Only the frame shows until the page is laid out, then everything fades in once. Column heights come from the pane, so a group that fills in later moves nothing outside its column.
  • Sending from a dashboard. The composer has the chat's width. On a desktop it moves down and toward the centre in one transform. On a phone it is already at the foot.
  • Entering the page puts focus in the composer.
  • The list cursor lands with this. ↑/↓ within a group, Tab to the next group, ←/→ between the columns, Enter opens, Esc returns to the composer.

Shape in code

  • DashboardShell becomes a container-queried grid with a header row and two columns, minmax(0, 608px) minmax(240px, 1fr), that turns into one column below 760px. DashboardGrid and the rail go.
  • DashboardIdentity → DashboardHeader, with the shortcuts as its trailing slot. The ⋯ moves to the pane header.
  • DashboardSection → DashboardGroup, which takes either a label or a segmented choice as its heading. DashboardRow loses the chevron and the second line, and gains a context span, a trailing counter and a hover action.
  • Not in Conduit replaces backend-sessions?implementation=codex with one request per harness that reports discovery, merged newest first, or with one server route that merges them. Track keeps the adopt route.
  • Changes reuses workspace-change-counts and the workspace panel's changes source. Files uses the folder listing the Files view already has. Terminals is today's Live terminals list, restyled.

Decisions for you

  1. Does the switch remember its side?

    Per workspace, or always opening on Chats. The sketch opens on Chats.

  2. What a click on a thread not in Conduit does.

    The sketch opens it as Drive does, with Track on hover. The alternative is to track it on click, so it opens as an ordinary Conduit chat.

  3. How far back Not in Conduit reaches.

    Everything, newest first, which is 150 today. Or the last 30 days by default, with the rest behind "Show all".

  4. Running takes live chats out of the threads list.

    The alternative leaves them in both places, marked live.
