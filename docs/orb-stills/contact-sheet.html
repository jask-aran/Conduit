Orb catalogue

Every frame the turn orb can draw: the thinking-orbs library's animations and the stills its own options hold them at, and the frames made for Conduit from the engine's pieces. Then each state a turn's header shows, with the orb it uses.

Prototype — the stop-start sphere, for Interrupted and Failed

The plain sphere in the destructive tint, turning in fits and starts rather than smoothly, from softest to most broken. Each enlarged, then as it would sit in both headers.

Library — animations

The 20px preset of each, at t = 0.3, 0.9, 1.5, 2.1, 2.7 and 3.3s, then playing.

Library — stills

The animations held or calmed by the library's documented options, at the moment Conduit shows a still, enlarged and at 20px.

Made for Conduit

Built from the engine's camera, dot scaling and depth sort, playing, enlarged and at 20px. At rest each is the still it was drawn as; the outlined card, at rest, is a settled turn's orb.

In the app

Each state of a turn's header, with its orb as it plays there: Done turns; Interrupted and Failed hold still. Listening is kept for dictation; breathing is unused.
