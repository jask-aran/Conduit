# Conduit v0.8.10

Conduit on Android gains Float: a frosted handle over any app that takes a
screenshot, a photo or your voice and sends it to jbot or a new chat, and that
answers a long press on home as the phone's assistant. Typing on a phone can
use a native composer that follows the keyboard frame by frame, under a live
blur of the transcript. Every library control has its own haptic, the
sidebar follows your finger, and jbot gains skills that load themselves, an
email routine and tools that reach conversations already running.

## Upgrading

- Update the Android app along with the server: Float, the native composer,
  haptics and the camera card are native. Installed clients bundle their own
  interface.
- Float is off until you turn it on in Settings › Phone, where one Set up
  walks through each permission it needs and comes back by itself.
  Screenshot needs screen context turned on.
- The native composer is Settings › Interface › Composer › Native while
  typing, off by default.
- Haptics have a strength in Settings › Interface › Haptics, per device.

## Float on Android

- Float is a small frosted handle at the screen's edge, over any app. A tap
  opens its radial: an arc of targets (jbot, a new chat, a workspace, recent
  chats) and what to send with it (a screenshot, a photo, screen context).
  A hold talks at once and locks onto a slot.
- What it holds shows as a capture card under the handle, with a count and a
  flash for each capture; a held capture can be discarded with its ✕.
- While it listens, Float glows with the composer's Aura, shows the words as
  they are heard in a frosted caption, and lowers other audio. Once sent it
  gathers into a bubble: a spinner, then a tick.
- Conduit can be the phone's assistant: a long press on home, or the side
  key, hands Float the screen and opens its radial, straight into dictation
  where you ask for it. Setup clears Circle to Search out of its way.
- On Samsung, Float's frost is One UI's live blur behind the handle, the
  radial's slots and the caption.
- Float opens the microphone off the main thread, draws the Aura a third as
  costly, and closes everything it opened when it stops.

## The phone composer

- Native while typing: the field, its pills and the edit pills are drawn
  natively while the keyboard is up, so opening and closing the keyboard is
  one motion with the composer and the transcript, on the keyboard's own
  curve. The keyboard's travel has no layout in it.
- On Samsung the native composer is live frost: the transcript blurs under
  every pill as it scrolls, from the composer's first frame, riding the
  keyboard up and down, and handing back to the web's pills in one frame.
- The transcript lands with the composer as the keyboard travels. Scrolled
  up, the transcript stays where it is while the keyboard comes over it.
- The composer's camera opens a picture-in-picture camera card; held, it
  opens the photo picker.
- A phone's composer dock and pane header have no ground of their own: the
  pills are frost over the transcript. A dictation's captions keep theirs.

## Haptics

- Haptics are tokens of the platform's clear primitives (tick, click, thud,
  settle, rise, knock), played natively, never as a timed buzz.
- Every control is felt by default and its `haptic` prop overrides it:
  Switch, Segmented, each Slider stop and a tapped list row tick; a primary
  Button confirms, a destructive one knocks, the others click. Menus,
  popovers, dialogs and the drawer are felt opening and closing, once per
  touch, and a phone's Settings section is felt arriving and going back.

## The phone sidebar

- The sidebar is a reveal drawer: the page slides 85% aside with your finger
  as the sidebar rises from under it, and lands the way you let go. It opens
  from 64px in, and it is felt as the drag crosses where it will land.
  Swipes that start on the composer don't open it.
- Closing it no longer opens the folder of the chat you are in, a popover
  can no longer leave it stuck halfway, folders near the list's end shut
  without a jump, and the touch scroll handle is gone.

## jbot and tools

- jbot's messages are their own page at `/jbot`, and its room and page are
  both in the sidebar.
- Skills are one block of what a role can load. Auto skills load themselves
  before the call that needs them, and connectors' own instructions reach
  the prompt.
- Email is a routine packaged with Conduit, off until you turn it on.
  Conduit sets up jbot's email in Cloudflare from one scoped token.
- jbot can be turned off. Its approval rules are a block the approval model
  and jbot both read; connected tools decide what is held.
- Tools that arrive later reach conversations already running. Prompts edits
  reach live threads at once, and no thread keeps a frozen prompt.
- Conduit's MCP server is `conduit`, with `conduit_ui` and `conduit_inbox`.
- Opening Conduit never opens the microphone: on a phone it took the audio
  and paused your music.

## Fixes

- A turn the live view lost settles, and a chat with no process reads idle.
- jbot's page loads before its routines' clocks exist.
- A dashboard row a click focused gains no wash when the window blurs.
